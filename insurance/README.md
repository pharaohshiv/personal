# Oscar 2026 plan

Gold Elite Saver Plus HMO $1000 $10. Plan ID 58081GA0010035-01. Oscar Health Plan of Georgia. Open Access network. Coverage 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

Key facts
- No referral required. In-network only; out-of-network covered only for emergencies and out-of-area urgent care.
- Deductible $1,000 medical, $200 prescription. Out-of-pocket max $8,500.
- Copays, deductible does not apply: PCP $10, specialist $25, labs $25, X-ray $75, MRI/CT $375, PT $25 (40 visits/yr, prior auth), urgent care $50, virtual urgent care $0.
- 30% after deductible: outpatient procedures (EGD), surgery, hospital, ER.
- Prior auth required: MRI (spine, pelvis, leg), physical therapy, inpatient. Not required: EGD, manometry, barium swallow, lumbar X-ray, ultrasound, lipoma excision, spirometry, labs.
- Weight-loss GLP-1s not covered (EOC exclusion 49). Chiropractic covered per EOC despite SBC listing.
- Non-emergency care abroad excluded. Buy travel medical insurance.
- Open enrollment Nov 1 to Jan 15. Reconsider for 2027 if the workup leads to ongoing procedures.
