# Speaking analysis (from the Alana consultation transcript, 2026-10-17)

Casual setting, so a floor not a ceiling.

Strengths
- Puts the other person at ease fast; volunteers trust and names awkward things before they're asked.
- Explains by concrete scene (the Friday aunties, the court diet, the airport return) rather than abstraction. Usable briefs.
- Frames decisions as bounded ranges ($150 to $250; "if the milk is $30, let's talk").
- Closes loops on the record ("did I forget anything?").

Patterns to notice
- The "allegories" are mostly hyperbole and running bits (villain, NPCs, the rapture, the bench). Anecdote speaker, not allegory speaker. Anecdotes are stronger with juries anyway.
- Filler density high: "you know," "you know what I'm saying," "like," "honestly," "obviously," "I feel like." "I feel like" turns a statement into an opinion and appears exactly when stating a preference or judgment.
- Sentences run on and pivot mid-thought, with the recovery announced ("oh, back to fridge management").
- Narrates his own quirks ("I've never had to say this out loud"). Honest, but signals uncertainty.
- Third person for emphasis ("when Shiv was in St. Lucia"), twice; a tell for mild embarrassment.
- Asks few questions; she asked more of him than he of her.

A cleaner version of one line: "I've been eating out at a level where food stopped meaning anything. Having someone cook plain, good food in my own kitchen is partly about getting that back."
