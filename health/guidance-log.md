- Diagnosis of the pattern: low muscle mass, fat at chest and waist, at 185 lb with daily training. South Asian visceral fat tendency. Cause is sleep phase and protein, not training.
- Three fixes in order: fixed wake time with daylight within 30 minutes; protein floor 140 to 150 g/day in four slots of 35 to 50 g; the ankle handled so training continues.
- Chest fat vs glandular tissue: exam decides. If glandular, tamoxifen is the drug; if fat, sleep and protein. Testosterone, estradiol, SHBG, prolactin on the next draw, 7 to 10 AM.

## Sleep (delayed sleep phase)
- Not insomnia. Fix wake time, not bedtime. Hold the alarm seven days a week, daylight immediately.
- At 1 AM when wide awake: keep working, lights low, protein snack not sugar, no caffeine after 4 PM, melatonin 0.5 mg four hours before target bedtime (set a reminder), go to bed when the wave hits around 2:30 to 3, never lie awake in bed.
- Expect 30 to 45 minutes of shift per week. Three weeks to move 1 AM from mid-evening to bedtime.

## Protein and supplements, practical
- No "30 g per meal" limit. Per-meal muscle response plateaus around 40 to 50 g; the rest still counts.
- Core Power 42 g (casein-heavy, slow) for wake-up and 2 AM slots. ISO100 (whey isolate, fast) after training, optional. Strawberry and chocolate are the safe flavors; mint chocolate chip and cookies-and-cream are the regret flavors across brands.
- ISO100 is already top tier (isolate plus hydrolysate, Informed Choice certified). Momentous and Thorne are the same whey at a higher price. Third-party testing is what matters, not brand.
- Clear whey: water first, powder second, 10-second shake, let sit 3 to 5 minutes, more water than the label. PROMiXX electric mixer or a $10 frother wand fixes clumps. Helimix is fine for regular whey, worse for clear whey and creatine.
- Creatine: 5 g daily, timing irrelevant, in the shake. Pills are 6 to 7 capsules; most people quit. Static in the powder is normal; metal spoon, liquid first.
- Mass gainer: no. 1,000+ kcal of maltodextrin feeds the fatty liver and the waist. Calories are not the shortfall; protein and timing are.
- Creatine plus sauna plus training: drink about a liter across the session. Sauna before lifting: keep it to 10 to 15 minutes.
- Electrolyte powder after sauna is appropriate; sugar content is 0 to 11 g and irrelevant. Stop drinking an hour before bed.
- Magnesium glycinate 200 to 400 mg, 30 to 60 minutes before bed, with the melatonin.
- Psyllium: always with a full glass of water, two hours from any pill, not right before training, morning slot.

## Heartburn, acute episode protocol
- ER if: pain to arm, jaw, or back; sweating or shortness of breath; pressure building past 15 to 20 minutes unrelieved by burping; cannot swallow water.
- Otherwise: stop drinking water (made it worse last time), stay upright two to three hours, Gaviscon or Tums for 15-minute relief, famotidine for the night, chew gum, loosen the waist, left side propped if sleeping. A PPI does nothing for the next several hours; don't double it.
- Log every episode: what, how much, delay, quality, duration, what relieved it. This episode type is why eosinophilic esophagitis and achalasia are on the GI list.

## Scalp
- Best read: oily scalp with yeast overgrowth (seborrheic dermatitis), made to look dry by washing 2 to 3 times a day. Gummy, waxy, sometimes wet scale in one spot is oil-pattern, not dry-pattern.
- Hair loss in one patch is not explained by dandruff. Three things to exclude by dermatology: psoriasis plaque, tinea capitis (needs oral terbinafine, shampoos won't cure it), scarring alopecia (permanent if delayed).
- Until then: wash once a day at most, Nizoral 1% twice a week on the scalp for five minutes, conditioner on lengths, oil overnight to lift scale, no scratching, no steroid until seen, weekly photo.
- Diffuse thinning on top is a separate process: male pattern loss. Finasteride 1 mg is the effective drug ($10 as quartered 5 mg); minoxidil foam or 2.5 mg oral after the patch is calm. Finasteride can cause breast tenderness in a few men, which matters given the chest question.
- Add ferritin and zinc to labs for diffuse shedding.
